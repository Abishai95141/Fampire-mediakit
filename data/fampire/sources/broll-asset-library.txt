SOP Clippers
🎬 SOP: Podcast Clip Editing for Reels & Short-Form Content
________________


1. Purpose of This SOP
This SOP defines the editing standards, visual rules, storytelling expectations, and approval criteria for clipping podcast episodes into short-form vertical videos (Reels/Shorts).
Our goal is to:
* Communicate ideas clearly and intelligently

* Maintain a premium, professional brand

* Ensure consistency across all clients and platforms

* Reflect the credibility of Biohack Yourself Media and our partners
⚠️ Attention to detail is non-negotiable. Any reel that does not meet these standards will not be approved.
________________


2. Reel Length & Structure
   * Ideal reel length: 30–60 seconds

   * Clips refer to the individual cuts that make up the reel

   * Reel refers to the final exported vertical video
Additional rules:
      * No clip should be under 3 seconds unless the original interview is cut that way and cannot be extended.

      * Reels must feel complete and intentional, not abrupt or confusing.

      * Avoid cutting mid-thought unless it improves pacing without harming clarity.

________________


3. B-Roll Requirements (Mandatory)
         * Minimum rule:
 👉 1 piece of B-roll every 10 seconds lasting 2-3 seconds minimum.

            * Example: 45s reel = minimum 4–5 B-roll inserts
            * Video is preferred over picture for b-roll. 

               * B-roll should:

                  * Reinforce what’s being said

                  * Add visual interest

                  * Never distract from the message
B-Roll Priority Order (Very Important)
                     1. A&T (Anthony & Tereza) B-roll

                     2. Documentary / event footage

                     3. Host-related B-roll

                     4. External or stock B-roll (only if absolutely necessary)
⚠️ If a clip is suggested, assume the footage exists. Look carefully before sourcing anything else.


________________


4. On-Camera Presence (Mandatory)
Every approved reel must include:
                        * ✅ The Host / Hosts
                        * ✅ Anthony & Tereza (A&T)
                        * ✅ Love & Legend if present on the podcast.
Do not create reels that exclude either unless explicitly instructed.
________________


5. Tagging & Instagram Handles
                        * Every person on camera must have their Instagram handle added on the first seconds of the reel, including:

                           * Hosts

                           * Guests

                           * Anthony & Tereza

                              * When A&T are speaking, ensure their handle is visible

                              * ❌ No handles over B-roll

                              * Handles should be:

                                 * Clean

                                 * Non-distracting

                                 * Consistently placed
________________


6. Subtitles, Captions & Text Rules
Typography
                                    * Keep font consistent across all reels

                                    * Font size must be easy to read on mobile

                                    * Avoid overcrowding the screen

Writing Standards (Critical)
                                       * Use proper grammar:

                                          * Question marks for questions

                                          * Periods at the end of sentences

                                             * No typos

                                             * No slang spellings

                                                * ❌ kickin’

                                                * ✅ kicking

                                                   * Sentences must make sense with or without sound
Emphasis & Highlighting
                                                   * Highlight key words sparingly, such as:

                                                      * Biohack

                                                      * Innovative

                                                      * Amazing

                                                      * Great

                                                      * Breakthrough
Name Accuracy (Very Important)
The following names must always be spelled correctly:
                                                         * Anthony Lolli

                                                         * TereZa Hakobyan Lolli

                                                         * Love Lala Lolli

                                                         * Legend Lolli
These are the four main figures behind Biohack Yourself and Lolli Brands.
________________


7. Logos, Branding & Placement
                                                            * Use the podcast logo from the host

                                                            * Logos should be placed on the sides of the video, not the bottom, to ensure visibility across all phone types

                                                            * ❌ Do not place logos at the bottom (they will be covered by captions and UI)

                                                            * Branding should remain subtle, clean, and professional
________________


8. Visual Composition & Technical Requirements
                                                               * Aspect ratio: 9:16 only

                                                               * Resolution: 1920 x 1080 (vertical 1080 x 1920)

                                                               * Pixels per video: 1920

                                                               * Highest quality possible is required

                                                                  * ❌ 720p is not acceptable

                                                                  * If only low-quality footage is available, you must alert us before exporting/clipping

                                                                     * No stretching, cropping, or misaligned framing

Images & Overlays
                                                                        * Any image that does not fill the screen must include:

                                                                           * Thin white or black border

                                                                           * Subtle drop shadow

9. Product Mentions & Client Awareness
Editors must be aware of Biohack Yourself Media partners.
Examples:
                                                                              * Therasage → Red light therapy

                                                                              * Celler8 → PEMF mats
Rules:
                                                                                 * Match visuals to the correct brand

                                                                                 * Use correct spelling and context

                                                                                 * Never misrepresent a product

If unsure, pause and ask before exporting.
________________


10. Music for Reels
                                                                                    * Background music is allowed and encouraged

                                                                                    * Music must:

                                                                                       * Be noticeable but never overpower the speaker

                                                                                       * Support the message emotionally

                                                                                       * Match the tone of the reel:

                                                                                          * Inspirational

                                                                                          * Dramatic

                                                                                          * Challenging

                                                                                             * Avoid:

                                                                                                * Corporate music

                                                                                                * Elevator-style music

                                                                                                   * Editors may use Envato (free options available) for music sourcing
Audio balance is critical — dialogue always comes first.
________________
11. Audio & Flow
                                                                                                   * Audio must be:

                                                                                                      * Clear

                                                                                                      * Balanced

                                                                                                      * Free of pops, harsh cuts, or clipping

                                                                                                         * Cuts should feel intentional, not rushed

                                                                                                         * Silence can be used briefly for emphasis
________________


12. Magazine Promotion (Mandatory for All Reels)
                                                                                                            * Every reel must end with a Biohack Yourself Magazine promotion

                                                                                                            * The promotion must appear at the end of the reel, never at the beginning or mid-way

                                                                                                            * The magazine promotion should last approximately 3 seconds

                                                                                                            * Use the most recent magazine advertisement only

                                                                                                               * Outdated magazine ads are not acceptable

                                                                                                               * If you are unsure which ad is the most recent, ask before exporting
The ad can be found in the newest edition of the magazine.
⚠️ This is mandatory for all reels.
 Any reel missing the magazine promotion, using an outdated ad, or not respecting the timing will not be approved.
    



________________


13. Quality Control Checklist (Before Submission)
Before submitting any reel, confirm all of the following:
                                                                                                                  * ⬜ 9:16 ratio = 1920 x 1080 (vertical 1080 x 1920) resolution

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Highest quality footage available used (720p is not acceptable)

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ If high-quality footage was unavailable, team was alerted before export/clipping

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Host and A&T appear on camera

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Required B-roll frequency met (1 every 10 seconds)

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ All on-camera people tagged with Instagram handles

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Names added in description if handle is not the person’s real name

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Correct spelling of all names
(Anthony Lolli, TereZa Hakobyan Lolli, Love Lala Lolli, Legend Lolli)

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ No typos or grammar errors in subtitles

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Logos placed safely on the sides of the frame

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Music supports the reel and does not overpower dialogue

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Most recent Biohack Yourself Magazine ad included

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Magazine promotion lasts ~3 seconds at the end of the reel

                                                                                                                  * ⬜ Overall message is clear, intentional, and professional

❌ If any box is unchecked, do not submit.
