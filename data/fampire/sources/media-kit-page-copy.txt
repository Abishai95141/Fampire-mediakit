PODCAST INTRODUCTION GUIDE FOR ANTHONY AND TEREZA LOLLI
Today’s guests are a couple who didn’t just survive the system &mdash; they rebuilt it.
Anthony and TereZa Lolli are the power couple behind Biohack Yourself Media &mdash; one of the fastest-growing independent health and wellness news platform in the world &mdash; reaching millions with expert-driven journalism, science-backed content, and award-winning storytelling.
B ut before they became health media disruptors&hellip; they were in survival mode.
Anthony Lolli is a real estate mogul and the best-selling author of The Heart of the Deal, who built a multimillion-dollar empire from scratch. But at 315 pounds, facing major health decline, he made the radical decision to document his 125-pound transformation on camera &mdash; launching the now-viral documentary Fat Lolli to Six Pack Lolli, which became a global phenomenon on Prime Video and Apple TV.
TereZa Hakobyan-Lolli is an Armenian-born actress, recording artist, mother, and editor-in-chief who turned postpartum hormonal collapse into a wellness revolution &mdash; winning two bikini world titles and documenting it all through films like The Super Lollis, Skin Deep, and sHEALed.
Together, they’ve co-founded Lolli Brands Entertainment &mdash; producing more than a dozen acclaimed health and transformation documentaries. And they’re raising two young trailblazers, Love and Legend Lolli, who are already reporting on health news, building media projects, and investing in real estate &mdash; all before age 10.
In 2024, their influence reached national heights when Biohack Yourself Media was selected as the exclusive health press at RFK Jr.’s MAHA Inaugural Ball.
They’re not just content creators &mdash; they’re immersive documentarians, performance marketers, and media entrepreneurs turning pain into purpose, and purpose into platforms.
This is what it looks like when you combine hustle, healing, and a whole lot of heart.

Let’s welcome Anthony and TereZa Lolli.

SYNOPSIS
Biohack Yourself is a five-part documentary from Lolli Brands Entertainment that follows Anthony and TereZa Lolli and their family as they explore the world of biohacking after facing life-changing health challenges and becoming disillusioned with the limitations of conventional medicine. Their journey introduces audiences to new possibilities for improving health, longevity, and human performance.
Featuring more than 25 evidence-informed wellness modalities, including red light therapy, hyperbaric oxygen, PEMF, nutritional therapies, and immersive health retreats, the documentary showcases technologies and approaches from leading innovators across the global wellness industry. Along the way, viewers gain insights from over 40 renowned physicians, scientists, inventors, and experts, including pioneers such as Dave Asprey and Ben Greenfield.
More than a documentary, Biohack Yourself serves as an accessible guide to the rapidly evolving world of biohacking. By combining personal storytelling with expert perspectives and practical innovation, the film empowers viewers to rethink what’s possible for their own health and discover new approaches to living longer, stronger, and better.
